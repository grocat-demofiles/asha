Our Story

Serving Sweet Memories Since 1951

What began in 1951 as a humble sweet stall in Malleshwaram has grown into one of Bengaluru's most trusted names in traditional sweets and gifting. Founded by the late Sri Kantha Prasadji Garg, the store was renamed Asha Sweet Center in 1971 by his son, Narendra Kumar Garg — a name that has stood for authenticity, quality, and warmth for three generations.

Today, our recipes remain rooted in tradition, made fresh every day using time-honoured methods and quality ingredients, while our packaging and gifting experience has evolved to meet the moment.
