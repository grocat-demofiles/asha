About Our Traditional Sweets

Asha Sweet Center has been handcrafting traditional Indian sweets in Bengaluru since 1951. Every Mysore Pak, Kaju Burfi, and Laddu in this collection is made fresh in small batches using pure ghee and quality ingredients, following recipes passed down through three generations of the Garg family. Whether you're picking up a box for daily indulgence or ordering a premium gift tin for a festival, our traditional sweets collection captures three generations of Bengaluru's favourite mithai tradition.
